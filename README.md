# ☁️ NoteCloud

**A simple place to write, organize, and share your notes.**

NoteCloud lets you keep your notes in one place and access them through your account. You can also collaborate with others when you need to work on something together.

---

##  Getting Started

### 1. Sign In

Open NoteCloud and click **Sign in with Google**.

Choose your Google account and you'll be taken directly to your NoteCloud workspace.

Your notes are connected to your account, so signing in with a different Google account will show that account's notes.

---

##  Creating a Note

Once you're inside NoteCloud:

1. Click **New Note**
2. Give your note a title
3. Start writing
4. Your changes are saved to your account

You can use notes for anything — class notes, ideas, meeting notes, to-do lists, project planning, or just random thoughts.

---

##  Editing a Note

To edit an existing note:

1. Open the note from your dashboard
2. Make your changes
3. Continue writing normally

Your updated note stays associated with your account.

---

##  Deleting a Note

If you no longer need a note:

1. Select the note
2. Choose **Delete**
3. Confirm the deletion if prompted

⚠️ **Be careful when deleting notes.** Only delete a note when you're sure you don't need it anymore.

---

##  Your Notes Are Private

Your NoteCloud workspace is connected to your Google account.

This means:

* Your notes belong to your account
* Other users cannot automatically see your notes
* Signing into another account gives you that account's workspace

---

##  Collaborating With Someone

Want someone else to work on a note with you?

1. Open the note you want to share
2. Select the **Collaborate / Share** option
3. Enter the other person's registered email
4. Send the collaboration request
5. The other person receives an invitation
6. Once they accept, they can access the shared note

Only people you choose to collaborate with can access the shared content.

---

## 📩 Accepting a Collaboration Request

If someone invites you to collaborate:

1. Check the email connected to your NoteCloud account
2. Open the collaboration invitation
3. Click the provided **Accept** option
4. Sign in to NoteCloud if required
5. The shared note will become available to you

---

## 🔄 Switching Accounts

If you want to use another Google account:

1. Sign out of NoteCloud
2. Sign in again with the other Google account
3. You'll see that account's notes

Your notes don't get mixed between accounts.

---

## Tips

* Use clear titles so your notes are easy to find.
* Keep related information in separate notes.
* Use collaboration when working on something with another person.
* Make sure you're signed into the correct Google account before creating or sharing notes.

---

##  Signing Out

When you're finished, use **Sign Out** from your account menu.

You'll be able to sign back in later with your Google account and continue using your notes.

---

### ☁️ That's it!

**Sign in → Write → Save → Share → Collaborate.**

NoteCloud keeps your notes simple, accessible, and connected to your account.
